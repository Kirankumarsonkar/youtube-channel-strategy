Audience research is the foundation of a successful YouTube channel. Understanding viewers helps create content that attracts, engages, and retains subscribers.

Viewer Behavior

Preferred Content Types

- Tutorials
- How-To Videos
- Reviews
- Tips & Tricks
- Educational Content

Viewing Time

- Morning: 7 AM – 10 AM
- Evening: 6 PM – 10 PM

---

Audience Pain Points

- Lack of reliable information
- Difficulty learning new skills
- Need for practical guidance
- Time constraints

---

Audience Goals

- Learn new skills
- Solve specific problems
- Stay updated with trends
- Improve career opportunities

---

Content Recommendations

- Create beginner-friendly tutorials.
- Use clear and engaging thumbnails.
- Focus on practical examples.
- Encourage audience interaction.

---

Conclusion

Understanding audience demographics, interests, and behavior helps create content that improves watch time, engagement, and subscriber growth.
